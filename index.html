About

Hello, I'm Felipe.

I look through the lens to freeze fleeting seconds, turning them into a visual superpower that floods me with memories every time I look back.

Specializing in the dynamic perspectives of drone photography, the immediacy of phone captures, and the precision of digital cameras, I translate the world as I experience it. My work also extends into the digital realm with three distinct projects on OpenSea.

When I'm not behind the camera, I channel my passion for strategy and teamwork as a soccer coach.
