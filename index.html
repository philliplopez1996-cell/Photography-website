About

Hello, I'm Felipe.

Born in Mexico and raised in the U.S. from the age of two, I've never quite felt rooted in one place. Moving often as a kid left me searching for a sense of home that never fully settled.

I grew up the youngest of four with a single mom who worked two jobs and no father figure in the picture. I'm the only one in my family who finished high school. I never went to college and never took a formal photography class — everything I've learned has come from simply doing it.

Today I work as a soccer coach and Uber driver. I still struggle to stay present, which is why I turn to flying my drone and hiking. Those moments in the air or on the trail are when I feel most alive.

My hope is to one day build a life around the things I love most. More than anything, I want to use what I create and what I do to help people and animals before my time is up.
